🐱 about

Data and AI, with the paperwork to prove it works

I'm an AI & Data professional based in Pune, working across data engineering, analytics and applied machine learning. The path here was not a straight line, and I think that's the point: I started in a Bachelor of Computer Applications, moved into enterprise systems and business intelligence work across the UK and India, then went back to do an MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Distinction, University of East London) to formalise the machine learning side properly.

That combination is basically my whole pitch. I've spent years in ERP data migrations, Power BI dashboards and Excel built for people who don't want to open Excel, so I understand how real organisations actually produce and mess up their data. My MSc then let me take that same instinct into deep learning, building and evaluating an LSTM model on real IoT sensor data to predict elevator failures before they happen (full write-up in Projects).

Since then I've kept building at the applied-AI layer: two AI-powered tools of my own, structured around the Claude API, and hands-on experience testing an AI healthcare chatbot for edge cases and failure modes. I'm not trying to pass myself off as a senior AI engineer. I'm early-career, genuinely curious, and I like the parts of the job that involve both spreadsheets and neural networks.

I try to stay properly current with AI, not just the polished announcements, but the trends, the debates, and the occasionally alarming bits too. I like getting hands-on with new models and tools to see what they can actually do, and where they quietly fall apart, which is basically how I ended up red-teaming a stranger's healthcare chatbot on LinkedIn (more on that in Beyond Work). Half curiosity, half mild chaos.
