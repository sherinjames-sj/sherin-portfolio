🧪 I Tried to Break an AI

Volunteer AI testing & red-teaming

A few months ago, I connected with someone on LinkedIn who was building an AI bot for the Australian healthcare space. I volunteered to poke at it, test its limits, and generally see where it would fall apart. It fell apart, a couple of times. What started as "let me see if I can break this" turned into hands-on AI testing and red-teaming: probing responses, finding edge cases, and figuring out where the bot needed to be more robust. Turns out breaking things can be a pretty useful skill.
